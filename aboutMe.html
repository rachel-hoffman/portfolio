About Me

Hi there, my name is Rachel!

I’m a UX designer who creates digital experiences that are intentional, intuitive, innovative, and inclusive. My background is in UX research and design, and I’m eager to gain a more holistic view of how digital products are built and maintained.

I took up coding seriously so that I could bring my own designs to life, and I ended up falling in love with how design and development inform each other. Now, I move between design and front-end code, using both to make interfaces that feel alive.
